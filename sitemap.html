Interactive Simulations

  • Conduction Simulation -

    A lattice of atoms which vibrate and transfer thermal energy through direct molecular interaction, demonstrating heat conduction principles.

  • Convection Simulation -

    A simple box of fluid which responds to heating and cooling, demonstrating thermal energy transfer through fluid movement.

  • Radiation Simulation -

    A glowing heater warms matte black and polished silver blocks across a vacuum by infrared radiation.

  • Buoyancy Simulation -

    Draw an object from materials of different densities and drop it into olive oil, water or mercury to explore Archimedes' principle and why objects float or sink.

  • Pressure Simulation -

    Watch gas particles strike the walls of a balloon, rigid container, syringe or weighted piston, and see how particle number, temperature and volume set the pressure.

  • Maxwell's Demon Simulation -

    A box of particles with a gate controlled by a demon, exploring entropy and the second law of thermodynamics.

  • Nuclear Decay Simulation -

    Build nuclei from protons and neutrons, collide them and watch real radioactive decays, from alpha and beta to fission, with half-lives and the chart of nuclides.

  • Special Relativity Simulation -

    Fly a spaceship past a space station at up to 0.999c and watch moving light clocks tick slowly, lengths contract and simultaneity change, with the twin paradox and cosmic-ray muons as real-world examples.

  • Orbits & Gravity Simulation -

    Drag stars, planets and moons, set their velocities and explore Newton's law of universal gravitation and Kepler's three laws.
